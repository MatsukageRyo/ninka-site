Privacy Policy

Kagi does not collect user data.

Data storage

SSH settings are stored locally on your device. Passwords and private keys are stored in iOS Keychain. Kagi does not operate a server that receives your SSH settings, passwords, private keys, terminal contents, or connection history.

No tracking

Kagi has no analytics, ads, tracking, or developer-operated data collection.
